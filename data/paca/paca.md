## Overview

Paca is an AI-native project management platform where AI agents and humans collaborate as Scrum teammates on the same board and sprints.

## Features

- AI agents as Scrum teammates
- Board and sprint management
- Configurable via config files
- WASM plugins
- Alternative to Jira, Trello, ClickUp
