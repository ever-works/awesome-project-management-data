## Overview

Quire is a super simple and stripped-down project management app with clean, customizable views and a freeform approach to task details.

## Features

- View options: tree, board, table, timeline, schedule, panorama, and calendar
- Toggle between views and pin favorites as tabs along the top of each project
- Freeform task details screen resembling a small Notion doc
- Slash commands for adding rich text, charts, tables, code blocks, and checkboxes
- Time entries with billable/not-billable toggle
- Peekaboo feature to snooze tasks until a selected timeframe
- Ready-to-use templates
- Fewer than 30 native integrations
- Zapier integration for connecting with thousands of other apps

## Pricing

- **Free plan**: Up to 4 projects, 10 members, nested list and Kanban views
- **Professional plan**: From $7.65/user/month, adds additional view options, up to 100 projects, time tracking, task dependencies, Peekaboo, and more

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS (Web, iOS, Android)

## Integrations

Fewer than 30 native integrations; Zapier integration connects with thousands of additional tools.
