## Overview

Hive is a simple yet scalable project manager with a minimalist, easy-to-navigate interface and extensive view customization options.

## Features

- Multiple view options: three Kanban variants, list, table, Gantt, calendar, and timeline
- Toggle between views, pin favorites, and set views to private
- Conversational AI assistant (Buzz) for summarizing projects, generating task descriptions and subtasks, and brainstorming
- Add-ons to extend the feature set (timesheets, proofing and approvals modules)
- Support for over 1,000 integrations including Zapier
- Recurring tasks and dependencies (paid plans)

## Pricing

- **Free plan**: Unlimited tasks, 10 projects, up to 10 members
- **Starter plan**: From $5/user/month (billed annually), adds unlimited usage of advanced views (Gantt, calendar, table) plus recurring tasks and dependencies

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS (Web, macOS, Windows, iOS, Android)

## Integrations

Over 1,000 integrations including Zapier for workflow automation.
