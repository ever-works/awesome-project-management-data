## Overview

Any.do is a task management platform that expanded into team project management with Any.do Workspace, balancing personal productivity with collaborative project features.

## Features

- Any.do Workspace for team project management
- Chat and file sharing for collaboration
- View options: Kanban, calendar, table, and Gantt
- Simple if/then automation
- My Day daily agenda builder for individual task focus
- Fewer than 20 native integrations
- Zapier integration for connecting with thousands of other apps
- Unlimited project boards and team members (Teams plan)

## Pricing

- **Free plan**: Personal task management and individual features
- **Teams plan**: From $4.99/user/month (billed annually), includes collaborative workspace, unlimited project boards and team members, plus advanced project management features

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS (Web, iOS, Android)

## Integrations

Fewer than 20 native integrations; Zapier integration connects with thousands of additional tools.
